What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Veravo, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over your data.

By creating an account and using Veravo, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

Data We Collect

Account Data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and send important service communications
  • Password — stored as a one-way cryptographic hash; we cannot read your password

Profile Information

During setup, you may optionally provide:

  • Your name
  • Household size
  • A financial goal description

This information is used only to personalize your experience within the app. It is not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Financial Data You Import

When you upload bank statement CSV files, we extract and store:

  • Transaction dates
  • Transaction descriptions (merchant names)
  • Transaction amounts (positive/negative)
  • The bank account label you assign (e.g., "Chase Checking")

We do not store raw CSV files. Files are parsed in your browser and discarded. Only the extracted transaction records are saved to our database.

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Bank account numbers
  • Routing numbers
  • Bank login credentials
  • Credit card numbers
  • Any information not explicitly contained in the CSV you upload

Budget Data

We store the budget line items, categories, savings goals, and payment method labels you create within the app.

Payee Pattern Contributions

When you assign a merchant to a category during import, your assignment contributes to an anonymized shared dataset called "global payee patterns." This dataset contains only:

  • A normalized merchant name string (e.g., "WHOLE FOODS")
  • A category name (e.g., "Food & Groceries")
  • A count of how many times this pairing has been confirmed

No personally identifiable information is stored in this dataset. There is no user ID, no account reference, and no way to trace a contribution back to any individual. This data is used solely to improve automatic merchant matching for all users.

You may opt out of contributing to global payee patterns at any time in your account settings. Opting out does not affect your personal matching rules, which remain private to your account.

Usage Data

We may collect limited technical information to operate and improve the service:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Pages visited within the app
  • Error logs

We do not use third-party advertising trackers. If we use analytics tools, we will update this policy to identify them and obtain appropriate consent.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide the budgeting features of the app
  • Match your transaction descriptions to budget categories
  • Show you your spending history and budget comparisons
  • Send you account-related emails (confirmation, password reset)
  • Improve automatic merchant matching through anonymized patterns
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues

We do not:

  • Sell your data to any third party
  • Use your financial data for advertising
  • Share your data with data brokers
  • Use your data to train external AI or machine learning models
  • Send you marketing emails without your explicit opt-in consent

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

  • Account data — retained for the life of your account plus 30 days after deletion
  • Transaction records — retained for the life of your account plus 30 days after deletion
  • Budget data — retained for the life of your account plus 30 days after deletion
  • Anonymized payee patterns — retained indefinitely as they contain no personal data

When you delete your account, all personally identifiable data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days. Anonymized payee pattern contributions cannot be deleted because they contain no information that identifies you.

If you would like us to delete your data before deleting your account, contact us at admin@ehigg.com.

Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Database access is restricted using Row Level Security — your data is isolated from other users at the database level and cannot be accessed by other users under any circumstances
  • Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth, which stores passwords as bcrypt hashes
  • Access to production infrastructure is restricted to authorized personnel only

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Veravo account.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and any relevant supervisory authorities as required by applicable law, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
